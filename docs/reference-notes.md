## 2. Camera

- **Wide shot** (announce, playback, wheel): front view from about chest height, slightly above the characters. Characters stand in a row in front of the back wall, the mic stand is in front of them at center, rugs/armchair/couch frame the sides. The camera drifts very slightly and never cuts hard.
- **Reveal**: same wide shot, but a dark semi-transparent overlay + blur, so the UI in the middle pops.
- **Countdown → recording**: a smooth dolly into **your** character (medium close-up, face big, neighbours partly in frame, logo on the wall behind). It pushes in a bit more between 3 → 1 (0:27 → 0:29, 2:27 → 2:29).
- **Playback**: back to the wide shot. On a great score (100) the camera punches in toward the mic with confetti (1:50).
- **Wheel**: the wheel sits over the room in the wide shot; after the spin the camera zooms onto the lower half of the wheel and the result text (1:16).

## 3. HUD / UI details

**Round box (top-left):** dark translucent rounded box, `ROUND 2 / 5` in pink chunky font, and the phase word under it in muted grey caps: ANNOUNCE / REVEAL / RECORDING / PLAYBACK.

**Player cards (top-center):**
- Fanned and overlapping, each slightly rotated.
- Coloured portrait header (player's colour), NAME in bold dark caps, **score as a big orange number** (no coin).
- Card order changes between rounds **(?)**, probably the playback order of that round.
- Hidden during REVEAL and RECORDING, visible in ANNOUNCE / PLAYBACK.

**Score during playback (the "number below" you asked about):**
- Big chunky text centered at the bottom, just under the mic: **`53 / 100`**, with a dark outline.
- Colour by score: **0 → pink/red**, **~50 → yellow/orange**, **80+ → green**.
- Small white line under it: **`Bonus: +20`**, `Bonus: +1`, **`Bonus: -5`** (negative bonuses exist too).
- The card's score updates after the number appears.
- High score = confetti burst over the whole scene (81, 92, 100).

**Names:** a white name label floats above every character. The active one gets a small white **▼** above the name.

**Voice chat indicator:** a green **🔊 speaker icon** floats above a character's head whenever that player is talking, in every phase.

**Bottom captions:** single line, white, centered: *"Round 3 – get ready, a sound is being picked…"*, *"Listen carefully to the demo"*, *"Everyone at once – one shot only!"*, *"GO!"*, *"Spinning…"*, *"Pick someone to sabotage!"*.

**Top-right:** **QUITTER / QUIT** button (dark red, muted).

**Announce (0:16):** 3 square icon buttons at the bottom center (mic icons: red-lightning mic, plain mic, gold swap mic). **(?)** Most likely the power-up inventory or a quick-action bar; they only show during announce.

**Reveal panel:** circular picture of the sound (meme/anime/game art), title in white chunky lower-case kebab style (`valorant-footstep`, `Weird laughing`), pack name under it in bold white, waveform panel (dark, cyan border, blue/white wave, red playhead), caption.

- Round "Roblox-y" toys: big head, gloves, simple outfits (plus a pink bunny onesie skin). Level avatars appear on the cards.
- **Faces are expressive:** eyebrows and mouth change (angry frown while recording, grin, sad).
- **Walking to the mic:** they walk out of the line with small **dust puffs** at the feet, stand *behind* the mic stand facing the camera, then walk back.
- **At the mic:** "singing" pose, hand up to the mouth like holding a mic (33:17).

### 5b. Sabotage in detail (re-checked frame by frame)

- **0:16, ANNOUNCE:** three power-up cards at the bottom center: **red-lightning mic** (sabotage), **plain silver mic**, **gold circular-arrows mic** (swap). They only show during announce.
- **1:16–1:21, WHEEL:** the phase word reads **WHEEL**. After the spin result, the power you won sits as an icon **top-left, over the round box** (gold swap mic at 1:19). The caption changes to **"Pick someone to sabotage!"** and you click a character in the wide shot. So the target is picked **right after the wheel**, not at the start of the next round.
- **RESULTS:** "Sabotaged takes: 4/20" counts them.
- The frames don't clearly show the effect on the sabotaged take. **(?)** It's audio-only, or too subtle to see at this resolution.

**Reference wheel (1:13, 2:09):** 10 segments. Star bubbles for the bonuses (+1, +5, ×1.5, +10, +20) and three **mic items labelled "x1"**: the red-lightning mic (**mic disturb**), the plain grey mic (**crap mic**) and the gold arrows mic (**swap**).

**Recording panel (0:29–0:34):** the demo wave sits in the middle band with dark margins on both sides. The red playhead only crosses the demo. The take (pink/white) paints over the blue as you speak and recording stops exactly at the end of the demo.

**Implemented in our game:**
- The wheel has 7 bonus stars + DISTURB / CRAP MIC / SWAP mic items (vector mic icons). In a solo game the mic items pay out +5 instead.
- CRAP MIC makes the take sound like a cheap broken mic (narrow band, crushed, clipped, hum + crackle). MIC DISTURB adds static + cut-outs.
- The recording window equals the demo length. The waveform is centered like the reference.
- The power bar during ANNOUNCE lights up the one you hold.
- The held power shows top-left.
- You pick the target right after the wheel.
- The victim sees "JAMMED" while recording (static + cut-outs).
- In PLAYBACK their character glitches, the caption says who sabotaged them, and a **⚡ SABOTAGED** stamp sits on the score.
